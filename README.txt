India TV - Sample Concept Website
==================================
Open index.html in any browser. Everything works offline (fonts and
images are bundled in assets/).

This is a design concept only. Every headline, story, image, video
and link is placeholder content, labeled "Sample" on the page.
"India TV" here is a fictional sample brand, not the real channel.

Built to match the sample logo and social banners: deep navy,
saffron/white/green tricolor accents, Poppins type.
